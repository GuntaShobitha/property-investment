Quick Help

How are dividends paid?

Dividends are disbursed quarterly to your registered bank account via wire transfer. Settlement occurs within 3 business days of declaration.

Can I withdraw my investment early?

Early withdrawal is subject to liquidity window availability and may incur a 2% early exit fee. Contact your portfolio banker for options.

How do I verify my accreditation?

Upload your net worth statement or income certification through the Documents section. Verification typically completes within 2 business days.
