The STACKLY Investment Journey

A transparent four-phase lifecycle designed to guide private capital from initial deal underwriting to ongoing quarterly liquidity.

01

Deal Sourcing

Our acquisitions committee screens over 250 off-market property packages quarterly, advancing only those meeting strict capital preservation criteria.

02

Due Diligence

03

Direct Allocation

Investors review detailed offering memorandums, select desired allocation amounts, and fund via segregated institutional escrow accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential considerations regarding our property selection criteria, syndication structures, and investor safeguards.

Minimum co-investment allocations typically range between $35,000 and $100,000 depending on the asset class and syndicate size. Institutional and family office tranches often commence at $500,000 or greater with tailored bespoke advisory agreements.

Every asset passes through a four-stage vetting committee. We commission independent RICS valuations, legal title searches by top-tier legal counsels, structural engineering audits, and proprietary demographic tenant demand simulations before structuring each syndicate.

Rental yields are disbursed quarterly directly to investor bank accounts or stored in their platform treasury balance. Upon asset refinancing or scheduled maturity liquidation, principal return and accrued capital appreciation are settled via regulated escrow accounts.

Yes. STACKLY structures syndicates through recognized special purpose vehicles (SPVs) that comply with cross-border tax treaties, facilitating participation for accredited investors in the UK, Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We maintain strict maximum debt-to-value (LTV) limits—generally not exceeding 55%—and maintain dedicated cash reserves for every asset. By acquiring properties in prime supply-constrained locations, our holdings exhibit superior historical pricing resilience.
