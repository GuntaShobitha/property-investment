Capital Preservation

Disciplined Risk Management & Downside Mitigation

We believe superior risk-adjusted returns stem from what an investor chooses not to buy. We reject 98% of off-market real estate submissions due to covenant flaws, over-leverage, or speculative exit assumptions.

Conservative Debt Caps (Max 55% LTV)

We protect equity capital against interest rate spikes by restricting senior debt and locking long-term fixed rates.

Ring-Fenced Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs)

Each property is held in an isolated legal structure. Liabilities from one asset can never impair another.
