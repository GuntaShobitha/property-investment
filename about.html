Our Origins

From Family Syndicate to Institutional Co-Investment Firm

Founded by property financiers and prime residential developers, STACKLY originated as a private syndicate acquiring heritage townhomes in central London.

Over two decades, our network expanded across Switzerland and France, syndicating Grade-A commercial headquarters and exclusive coastal hospitality estates. Today, we empower over 300 private investors and institutional partners with institutional-grade property stewardship.
